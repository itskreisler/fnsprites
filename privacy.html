Privacy Policy

This is an unofficial fan-made tracker. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Epic Games.

Local storage

Your collection is stored only in your browser on this device. We do not have access to it and do not collect or transmit any personal data.

Google Drive sync

Optional: you can enable Google Drive sync to back up your collection. The backup is a JSON file saved in your own Google Drive account, created by this app with limited access.

Third-party services

When using the optional Drive sync or the "Continue with Google" option, Google processes authentication data under its own privacy policy.
